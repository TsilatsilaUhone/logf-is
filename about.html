We walk with families through hardship.

Life of the Giving Foundation began with a simple commitment: when people are struggling, we show up with practical care, dignity, and hope.

What we do

Food support

We distribute food parcels, school meals, and emergency packs to households facing hunger and crisis.

Youth care

Through mentoring, after-school clubs, and life-skills programmes, we support young people to thrive.

Emergency response

When disaster strikes, we coordinate rapid assistance for families who need urgent shelter, essentials, and counselling.

Our story

From a small volunteer response in Gauteng, our organisation has grown into a trusted local network of partners, community workers, donors, and caregivers. We believe that lasting change begins where people already live, work, and worship.

We support communities through compassionate service, careful coordination, and a deep respect for the people we serve.

Our values

Compassion

We meet people with empathy and respect, recognizing their dignity in times of vulnerability.

Integrity

We act transparently, responsibly, and with accountability in every decision.

Community

We strengthen neighbourhood support systems and help people reconnect with practical care.
