COMMON QUESTIONS

Field operations help

How do I sign in?

Use an account provisioned by the foundation administrator. Access is limited to authorized staff.

How do I raise an emergency alert?

Open Emergency Alerts, describe the situation, choose its severity, and provide the location. Alerts are logged in Firestore but are not sent to responders.

How do I record an assessment?

Save a beneficiary first, open Assessments, select the beneficiary, record findings and a follow-up outcome, then save.

How do I log a relief distribution?

Save a beneficiary first, then open Relief log. Select the beneficiary, choose the item, enter the quantity, and save the record.

Where are records saved?

Beneficiary, assessment, alert, and distribution records are saved in Firestore and visible to authorized staff.
