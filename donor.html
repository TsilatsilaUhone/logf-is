Share your pledge and the foundation will contact you to confirm a verified way to give. This form records your pledge; it does not process a payment.

Ways to give

One-time gift

Make a direct contribution to support urgent community needs across Gauteng.

Monthly giving

In-kind support

Donate goods, school supplies, blankets, or essential household items for local distribution.

How donations are used

  • Food baskets and emergency food relief for households in crisis.
  • Transport, supplies, and mobile support for outreach teams.
  • Youth and counselling services that help families regain stability.
