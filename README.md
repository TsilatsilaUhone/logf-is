# Life of the Giving Foundation

The root `index.html` is the Field Operations app. It includes beneficiary intake, needs assessments, emergency alerts, relief distribution, and donation information. Staff sign-in uses Firebase Authentication, and operational records sync with Firestore.

Firebase project configuration is in `web/firebase-config.js`; Firebase Hosting and Firestore deployment settings are in the root `firebase.json`. Enable Google and Email/Password providers and upgrade to Firebase Authentication with Identity Platform to enable SMS multi-factor authentication. Configure the SMS region policy and authorized domains. Staff must verify their email, enroll a phone with SMS, and sign in again before the workspace opens. Firestore rules also require Firebase's `firebase.sign_in_second_factor == "phone"` token claim. Main-page registration creates an email-verified account but grants no staff role; an administrator must provision `role: "field_worker"` (existing accounts with `staff: true` remain supported) or `role: "admin"` through a trusted Admin SDK environment. After the first successful field-worker sign-in on a browser, the user is redirected to `community.html`; the browser remembers this per account, and later sign-ins open the operations workspace. Beneficiaries, assessments, alerts, distributions, and donation pledges are stored in Firestore. Assessment records are linked to an existing beneficiary and are immutable after creation. The separate sign-in screens currently open the same operations workspace; admin-only workspace features are not defined yet. Analytics runs on the public pages and field operations app. The contact form opens the visitor's email app and does not store messages in Firestore. Deploy rules and site with `firebase deploy --only firestore,hosting`. Alerts are logged in Firestore but are not sent to responders, and the website does not process donation payments.

The donor portal stores donation pledges (donor name, email, amount in ZAR cents, frequency, contact consent, pledge status, and server timestamp) in the Firestore `donations` collection. Staff can view pledges in the operations app's Donations page. This is a pledge and follow-up request only: the site does not charge donors or confirm payment, and no bank or payment details are configured. Deploy the Firestore rules with `firebase deploy --only firestore:rules` to enable public pledge submissions and staff-only reads.

`community.html` preserves the earlier public home page. Open it for the original community introduction.
